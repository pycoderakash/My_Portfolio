About Akash Rai

Student Developer pursuing a Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science and Engineering (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning).

Background & Learning Journey

I am a student developer currently pursuing my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning) under the curriculum of Chhattisgarh Swami Vivekanand Technical University (CSVTU), Bhilai.

My learning is driven by a passion for practical problem-solving. Rather than studying concepts purely in theory, I actively build software applications to understand how technologies work end-to-end—from parsing unstructured text documents with regular expressions in Python to designing secure client-server API workflows for AI models.

I am particularly fascinated by the intersections of Artificial Intelligence, Generative AI, and full-stack software development. Through coursework and self-directed projects, I explore how algorithmic techniques, modern web frameworks, and responsible system design come together to build tools that assist real users.

Development Philosophy

"Building practical software through honest iteration: understanding foundational data structures, respecting security boundaries, and designing accessible interfaces that work reliably for everyone."
